A trip is not a new relationship schedule. It is a week with a different clock, a hotel room, and worse sleep. The shared alarm should get simpler for those days, then return to the standing plan when someone is home.

This is for a short trip, not a standing long-distance routine. If you live apart, start with the [long-distance morning playbook](/blog/long-distance-couple-morning-routine). If one of you rotates clinical shifts, use the [travel nurse guide](/blog/travel-nurse-partner-alarm-routine) instead.

## Decide before the flight

Write three lines in the notes app and send them:

1. **Home plan:** paused or still on for the person who stays.
2. **Trip window:** the one overlap that is still fair, in both local times, or "no shared morning until I land home."
3. **Resume day:** the date the standing alarm turns back on.

The American Academy of Sleep Medicine recommends that adults regularly sleep 7 or more hours. A trip that already cuts sleep should not add a shared 5 a.m. call to prove the routine still exists. A voice note after one of you is up is a complete version of "we are still in this."

## The person who stays

Their alarm can stay on if their life did not change. Label it so it is obviously the home plan, not a call the traveler is expected to join. Do not text "you missed it" into a time zone where the other person is at dinner.

## The person who flies

Hotel nights are a placement problem. The phone on the charger, not under a pillow. Sound not set to None. Apple says Clock alarms still sound in Silent mode, and StandBy turns alarm haptics off, so check the sound if the phone is a bedside clock.

For the first two mornings, treat the alarm as personal. Jet lag is not a character issue. Rejoin the shared window on the resume day, or pick a new one if the trip showed the old time was fantasy.

If the destination observes a different daylight-saving rule, run the [daylight-saving checklist](/blog/daylight-saving-shared-alarms) the evening you arrive. Set Automatically should be on, with Setting Time Zone enabled, so the phone can follow the new zone. Apple says manual clock edits can affect alarms.

## What to skip

- A shared challenge at 3 a.m. body time "so we don't break the streak."
- Two alarms an hour apart "just in case" that you forget to delete.
- Using location sharing as the travel check-in. A message is enough, and it is the privacy boundary in [shared alarm privacy](/blog/privacy-of-shared-alarm-schedules).

## FAQ

### Should we keep the same wake time in both cities?

Only if both local times are still reasonable. Otherwise pause the shared plan and keep a personal alarm for the traveler.

### When do we turn the shared alarm back on?

On the resume day you named before the flight, after both people have slept at home.

### Does SyncUpAlarm replace a travel itinerary?

No. Keep flights on the calendar. Use the alarm for the wake you still want.
