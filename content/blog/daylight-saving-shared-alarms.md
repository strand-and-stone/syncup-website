Per NIST, US daylight saving time ends at 2:00 a.m. local time on Sunday, November 1, 2026, when clocks fall back one hour, so the day has one extra hour. Apple Support says iPhone adjusts for daylight saving time when Set Time Automatically is on, and Android Help describes an Automatic date and time setting. If you share a wake time with someone, use the checklist below the night before.

## When daylight saving time ends in 2026

Per NIST: DST is in effect from March 8 at 2 a.m. to November 1 at 2 a.m. (local time) in 2026. At 2:00 a.m. on the first Sunday of November, local time goes back to 1:00 a.m. ("fall back"), so that day has one extra hour.

## Where clocks don't change

- Per NIST, DST is not observed in Hawaii, American Samoa, Guam, Puerto Rico, the U.S. Virgin Islands, or Arizona.
- The exception is the Navajo Nation (NIST says "Navajo Indian Reservation"), which does observe DST.

## What Apple and Google document about phone clocks

Apple and Google don't spell out alarm behavior at the time change on these pages, so open your alarm list the night before and confirm the times read the way you expect.

### iPhone

Per Apple Support, "Use Date & Time settings to have your device adjust automatically for time zone changes and daylight saving time." Path: Settings > General > Date & Time > Set Time Automatically. Apple also documents Settings > Privacy & Security > Location Services > System Services > Setting Time Zone. If a message says updated time zone information is available, Apple's step is to restart the device and any paired Apple Watch.

[iPhone alarms complete guide](/blog/iphone-alarms-complete-guide)

### Android

Per Android Help: Clock app > Settings > Change date and time > Automatic date and time on. Under "Time zone," turn on Automatic time zone.

## When one partner's clock doesn't change

Until Nov 1, Denver is on daylight time and Phoenix isn't, so Denver is one hour ahead of Phoenix. After clocks fall back on Nov 1, both show the same clock time. Reason: Denver falls back and Arizona doesn't change, per NIST.

## What not to do

Don't turn off automatic time just to "fix" an alarm. Apple Support says manual adjustments to time settings to change app behavior aren't recommended.

## FAQ

### When does daylight saving time end in 2026?

Per NIST, US daylight saving time ends at 2:00 a.m. local time on Sunday, November 1, 2026, when clocks fall back one hour to 1:00 a.m.

### Does "fall back" mean I gain an hour?

Yes. Per NIST, at 2:00 a.m. local time the clock goes back to 1:00 a.m., so that day has one extra hour.

### Which places don't change their clocks?

Per NIST, daylight saving time is not observed in Hawaii, American Samoa, Guam, Puerto Rico, the U.S. Virgin Islands, or Arizona, except the Navajo Nation, which does observe it.

### How do I make sure my iPhone adjusts for daylight saving time?

Per Apple Support, go to Settings > General > Date & Time and turn on Set Time Automatically, and turn on Setting Time Zone under Location Services > System Services.

### How do I set Android to update the time on its own?

Per Android Help, open the Clock app, go to Settings > Change date and time, and turn on Automatic date and time. Under "Time zone," turn on Automatic time zone.

### What should two people sharing a wake time check the night before?

Both phones use automatic time, the shared alarm's time and label read as expected, and anyone in a place that doesn't change knows the new hour gap.
